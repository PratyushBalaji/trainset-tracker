## Opening-count discipline

The Timeline now scores Blue in `reviewedLineStories` as five separate histories:
whole line, ORR (2A), whole Phase 2B, Hebbal - Airport and KR Puram - Hebbal.
Broad periods take the latest day they could cover (2023-24 as end-2024); early
2028 is only ordered after December 2027 and is never shown as a day. The 2021
ORR date is scored as retrospective, and conflicting reports are left unscored.
The histories must not become one automatically inferred chain.

- Whole-airport and whole-line promises are not assigned retroactively to later
  airport subsections, or counted once for each subsection.
- Construction schedules and trial-start deadlines are not passenger-opening
  promises. June 2024 civil work and June 2025 commissioning targets in the
  January 2023 proceedings remain distinct from public opening forecasts.
- Reaffirmations are not new missed promises. This includes June 2026 ORR,
  October trials, June/December 2027 airport forecasts and May 2027 ORR.
- September 2025 to December 2024 airport reporting is an advance, not an
  extension. Do not force all target histories to increase monotonically.
- November 28 and 29, 2024 give conflicting airport schedules; October 2026
  reports conflict on mid-2027 versus end-2027 airport connectivity. Retain the
  attribution and scope rather than declaring an agreed replacement date.
- Changed future targets are superseded, not already missed at the cutoff.
- A later section-aware scorecard must support imprecise/current forecasts
  without inventing days or silently interpreting early 2028 as March 31.
