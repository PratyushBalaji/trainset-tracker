fresh independent source audit. Line events live in `lines.json`. No individual
trainset records or schema changes are part of this import.

## Deadline discipline and held claims

No Green delay scorecard is added. Each stage must eventually be measured
against its own passenger opening. Shared Phase 1 completion promises belong to
the project and must not also be counted as independent Green/Purple promises.
Civil-completion targets, unofficial forecasts, disputed exact-day readings and
achieved ceremony/service announcements need separate treatment.

Records with supported evidence dates appear in the feed. Retrospectives keep
their publication date and explicitly label the older targets they recall;
those are not presented as original announcements. The following lack a safe
plotting date or need original corroboration:

- Original Phase 1 schedules: June 2011 for the initial DPR and June 2012 for the
  enlarged project in [CAG's report](https://d1jmc0u14z2tmp.cloudfront.net/uploads/download_audit_report/2026/CAG-Report-No.-7-of-2026-%28English%29-06a79ba08602d89.76651635.pdf).
  Original announcement/revision dates are unknown. Later December 2012/2013
  baselines are attributed in the dated retrospective/parliamentary records,
  not collapsed into one supposedly agreed original deadline.
- Peenya - Nagasandra: December 2014/January 2015 forecast in
  [DH reporting](https://www.deccanherald.com/content/434612/metro-begins-trial-run-peenya.html).
  Exact publication date needs confirmation. Do not invent a trial-start date
  from that forecast; the section opened on 1 May 2015.
- Madavara's original 27-month civil period, mid-2019 descriptions and August
  2019 reported opening need the original agreement to reconcile. Preserve the
  duration separately from the opening month.
  [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/india/3-km-in-7-years-bengaluru-metros-most-delayed-section-ready-for-commercial-operations-gets-cmrs-nod-12835794.html),
  [TNIE](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/karnataka/2024/Jul/31/bangalore-metro-to-start-trial-runs-on-nagasandra-madavara-line-from-august-6).

  The document's exact evidence date is not established.
- Madavara: December 2023 in secondary reporting attributing a year-end forecast

  [Oneindia](https://www.oneindia.com/bengaluru/baiyappanahalli-krishnarajapuram-metro-line-to-be-operational-in-september-3611815.html).

These target dates passed, but missing original evidence dates are not supplied
by backdating the report to the target itself. Older southern-extension targets
and Phase 1 revisions with a known retrospective report are preserved as such
in the feed rather than assigned invented announcement dates.

## Disagreements and limits

- The original state approval and several Reach 3/3A/4/4A contract dates remain
  unknown. Verified Reach 3B dates do not establish those other packages.
- November 2016 tunnel transfer was battery-locomotive hauling, not powered
  mainline testing. November 18/19 is disputed; only the month is plotted.
- Underground powered trials: March 30 versus March 31, 2017. Only March is
  plotted, with both candidate days in the evidence note, not a fabricated
  two-day trial-duration interval.
- Final Phase 1 CMRS inspection: May 29-June 1 versus agency accounts of three
  days from May 29. The reported four-day interval is flagged as disputed.
- CMRS signed on June 7, 2017; June 8 was the reported scheduled handover. Do not
  turn scheduled handover into a separately confirmed receipt event.
- Silk Institute CMRS inspection was November 18-19, 2020, not December.
  November 23 authorisation carried 37 conditions.
- Madavara trials: August 6 was planned and later claimed retrospectively;
  contemporary Indian Express coverage says August 17. Only August is plotted;
  no unsupported split into two testing stages is invented.
- Madavara contractor replacement/termination is not added. Retrospective claims
  conflict with contemporary final-segment reporting crediting Simplex; original
  contract documents are needed.
- November 6, 2024 was a ministerial inspection ride, not automatically a formal
  inauguration. November 7 public opening is supported by contemporary records.
  The conflicting later November 17 summary remains in the opening evidence
  note and must not replace November 7.
- Exact 2014 CMRS dates, original RDSO clearances, separate ISA certificates,
  whole-section track/electrification completion and complete sanction conditions
  remain incompletely established. Particular tests do not supply missing
  completion-certificate dates or prove individual trainset induction.
