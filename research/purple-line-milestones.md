## Evidence precision

- DPR preparation in March 2003 and submission in May are separate events.
- State Cabinet approval (3 March 2005), government order (24 March), Union
  approval (25 April 2006) and communicated sanction (11 May) remain separate.
- The 2006 foundation stone is ceremonial; physical Reach 1 work started
  15 April 2007. Navayuga's 27 July 2010 package completion excludes stations
  and systems and is not line completion.
- First mainline testing: December 2010 is established, definitely by December
  28. December 27 is an eyewitness-supported candidate, not a settled first-ever
  date. Both source links remain with the month-only record.
- Reach 1 RDSO clearance is established by 14 August 2011, not issued exactly
  on that reporting day. A fax had arrived; the formal report was still expected.
- Underground first movement was 12 March 2015, supported by next-day coverage.
  Only the initial section was electrified; onward transfer needed battery
  assistance. Both-track dynamic testing was established by 11 November.
  Neither November 23 nor a retrospective March 13 replaces those records.
- Reach 2 safety clearance: September 2015 is established, but September 19 and
  issuance/receipt timing remain disputed. The exact day stays unset.
- Underground CMRS inspection actually ran 5-6 April 2016. Earlier three-day
  plans do not replace completed-inspection evidence. Conditional clearance was
  received by 8 April; the issue date remains unknown.
- Eastern civil awards were established by May 2017, a month-level bound rather
  than a May 1 event. March's lowest-bidder announcement is not the award date.
- RINA's September 2019 announcement establishes assessor engagement, not
  completed ISA certification or a confirmed signing day.
- Kengeri CMRS inspection: 11-12 August 2021, not August 10; authorisation was
  16 August, supported by the CRS annual report and contemporary coverage.
- Whitefield trials actually began 21 October 2022, before the planned October
  25 date. That is not a missed testing deadline.
- March 2023 one-track operation near KR Puram reflects a missing crossover;
  it is not automatically a condition imposed by CMRS.

## Target and counting discipline

The Timeline scores Purple per opening part (Reach 1, Reach 2, the underground
link, Kengeri, the eastern extension and Challaghatta); readings live in
`src/lib/data/reviewed-targets.ts`. Retrospective targets are scored but carry no
announcement date. Target text remains precise to the evidence:
months, quarters, partial-section proposals, ceremony dates and passenger dates
are not interchangeable. Existing exact-date scorecard mappings do not establish
historical announcement dates that the research has not recovered.

Each retrospective target has its own record under the supported report date,
with the original announcement date explicitly unknown. In particular, the
Kengeri sequence December 2018, March/November 2019, October 2020,
February/April/June 2021 must not be plotted as announcements on those target
dates. The June 23, 2021 evidence date precedes June month-end: June's eventual
miss is later historical context, not a miss already established on June 23.

Keep the following apart when section-aware scoring is added:

- Original western and underground opening programmes versus selected-station
  May 2015 partial-opening proposals.
- Reach 2 December 2014 civil completion versus February 2015 passenger service.
- Kengeri's proposed 20 August 2021 inauguration versus passenger-opening
  promises; its achieved August month target is measured against August 30.
- Official December 2022 whole-eastern-extension target versus the March 2023
  source forecast. March's partial KR Puram - Whitefield opening did not fulfil
  a whole Baiyappanahalli - Whitefield promise.
- Final-links proposed October 2/6 launches versus achieved October 9 passenger
  opening and the later October 20 ceremony.
- Shared Phase 1 promises already retained with project-wide scope in Green
  research. Do not count Green's remaining delays against Purple after its
  original corridor opened on 30 April 2016, or duplicate a shared promise once
  per line in project totals.

The consolidated recovered history is not proof that every promise has been
found. Package completion, tunnel boring, tracks, station fit-out and operating
readiness remain distinct; missing original records remain missing.
